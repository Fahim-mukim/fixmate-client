# FixMate Brand Guidelines — v0.2

## Brand essence
FixMate is a smart-device and home-appliance repair marketplace connecting customers with technicians. The identity should communicate trust, practical expertise, and modern convenience.

## Logo concept
Minimal Smart Home + Shield + Wrench.
- House: home and appliance context.
- Shield: trust and protection.
- Wrench: repair expertise.
Keep the mark simple at small sizes. The current SVG is a clean vector draft and should be visually reviewed before final production approval.

## Approved direction vs working choices
- Selected direction: Option C, Modern Tech × Cinematic Editorial.
- Selected symbol concept: Smart Home + Shield.
- Working palette: Navy #101C2C, Teal #17B8A6, Amber #F4A340, Mist #EAF2F5.
- Typeface candidate: Plus Jakarta Sans.
- Working tagline: “Smart Repair. A Better Tomorrow.”
These are current design decisions, but accessibility and assignment review still apply.

## Logo use
Use `fixmate-logo-light.svg` on light surfaces and `fixmate-logo-dark.svg` on dark surfaces. Use `fixmate-mark.svg` or `fixmate-icon.svg` for compact placements. Never stretch, skew, rotate, outline, add drop shadows, or change the colors. Keep clear space around the shield approximately equal to the roof-stroke thickness multiplied by four.

## Voice
Clear, reassuring, concise, and respectful. Avoid unsupported claims such as guaranteed same-day service or 24/7 availability unless confirmed by project requirements and real service operations.
