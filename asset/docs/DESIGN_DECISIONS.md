## Refinements made
- Simplified the logo geometry and reduced visual clutter.
- Removed CTA buttons from hero and social banner artwork.
- Reduced neon/glow effects and emphasized whitespace, typography, and contrast.
- Defined separate light and dark surface tokens.
- Added service-category line icon set.
- Clarified that real interactions must be implemented as frontend components, not baked into image assets.
